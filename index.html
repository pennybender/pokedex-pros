    How to play

    The basics

    Pick a difficulty and the categories you want, then answer before the timer runs out. Every level has seven questions, and the questions get tougher as you climb.

    Lives

    A wrong answer or a timeout costs a life. Clear a level and you win one back. The game ends when you run out.

    Points

    Harder questions are worth more. Fast answers earn a speed bonus and a run of correct answers builds a streak bonus. Clearing a level pays a bonus too — bigger if you got all seven right.

    Your trivia team

    Build a team of up to six Pokémon. Tap a teammate during a question and they'll tell you what they think the answer is. Each one can help once per level.

    They're honest about how sure they are. A teammate is certain about questions on its own evolutionary family, confident about its own types, and only guessing on everything else — so a balanced team covers more ground.

    Install it

    On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android: menu → Install app. It then works offline.
